Handoff checklist

Core setup is complete; ownership and acceptance remain. Production authentication, organization-owned data, isolated previews, CI cleanup, and the new Unity build account are configured. The website's develop branch feeds production; switching branches is optional future work.

Health and sign-in contrast fixes are live. Storage cleanup removed 32 unused previews; Preview retention is now 7 days, with Production kept at 30 days. Refreshed storage usage still needs checking.

Functional testing was reported successful by the project lead. Confirm incoming-owner access, test outage alert delivery, and record accepted device coverage and client sign-off. Backup verification remains excluded and unverified.

The classroom experience

States of Matter teaches heating, cooling, and changes of state through Matter Kitchen, Pipe Rescue, and State Lab. Penguin Run teaches gravity, friction, and potential energy through three track-building levels.

  1. An educator signs in and opens a named class. Learners join with a display name and the shared class code; a personal account is optional.
  2. Each learner takes the pre-quiz, plays the game, and finishes the post-quiz. Educators confirm results in their dashboard or class history.
  3. Codes last eight hours. Reopen an existing class to continue its history and share the new code. Guest learners may get a new identity when they rejoin.

Keep one learner per browser profile during a session. Use an internet connection and test the actual classroom devices. Saved website records do not guarantee offline or cross-device game recovery.

What the results mean

A score change from 40% to 60% is 20 percentage points. Missing quizzes are not zeroes. Admin averages weight classes equally, and pre/post averages may contain different learners. Report scope and missing results; do not describe these figures as proof of individual learning gains. The partner guide explains the limits.

Make the transfer concrete

  • Name owners and backups: partner support, repositories, Vercel hosting, MongoDB, Clerk, Unity licensing, learner data, and billing. Test the incoming team's access.
  • Demonstrate the work: a fresh developer setup, both classroom game/quiz loops, a failed-save retry, guest rejoining, and a second learner using a shared device.
  • Demonstrate operations: identify the live game versions, review the successful new-account builds, rehearse a safe post-cutover rollback, and deliver an alert to the named responder.

Record the accepted release, evidence, limitations, and follow-up owners in the handoff checklist. Contact names and account details remain unassigned until the teams fill them in.
